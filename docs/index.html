What the match score means

The score is a relevance estimate, not a hiring probability. It weighs the job title, technologies and responsibilities found in the source, preferred geography and seniority. A high score is a signal to review the vacancy, not a substitute for checking mandatory experience, work authorisation or employer eligibility.
